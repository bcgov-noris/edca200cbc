The Canadian Broadcasting Corporation (CBC)

90 years of adult education over the airwaves

Key dates

1932
The Canadian Radio Broadcasting Commission (CRBC) is formed
1936
The CRBC is renamed the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation (CBC)
1943
Citizens' Forum goes on the air, based on popular Farm Radio Forum
Mid-1950s
Both radio forums end as participation dwindles
1965
Ideas radio program first airs, first topic: evolution
2026
Ideas airs the 5-part Massey Lectures by Leilani Farha, starting Nov 16
